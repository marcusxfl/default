## Chemical Reaction Model

**1. Describe key characteristics of all chemical reactions, including the role of energy. Explain how a balanced equation represents these features (include an example).**

Key characteristics of all chemical reactions:
- **Conservation of mass/atoms**: Atoms are rearranged, not created or destroyed. The same number of each type of atom must appear on both sides of the equation.
- **Conservation of charge**: Total charge is conserved between reactants and products.
- **Bond breaking and forming**: Reactant bonds break (requires energy) and new product bonds form (releases energy).
- **Energy change**: Every reaction absorbs or releases energy. If more energy is released in forming bonds than is used to break bonds, the reaction is **exothermic** (releases energy). If the opposite, it is **endothermic** (absorbs energy).
- **New substances**: Products have different chemical properties from reactants.

A balanced equation represents these features by showing equal numbers of each atom on both sides (conservation of mass), correct formulas/charges (conservation of charge), and an energy term on the appropriate side.

**Example (exothermic combustion of methane):**

$$CH_4(g) + 2\,O_2(g) \rightarrow CO_2(g) + 2\,H_2O(g) + \text{energy}$$

- C: 1 = 1 ✓   H: 4 = 4 ✓   O: 4 = 4 ✓
- Energy appears on the product side → exothermic.

### Reactions with Energy Terms, Energy Bar Charts, and PE Graphs

**4. 3 M HCl added to solid sodium carbonate — bubbles and gets warm. Products: CO₂(g), H₂O(g), NaCl(aq).**

Balanced equation (exothermic — test tube warms):

$$2\,HCl(aq) + Na_2CO_3(s) \rightarrow 2\,NaCl(aq) + H_2O(g) + CO_2(g) + \text{energy}$$

- **Type of energy change: EXOTHERMIC** (energy is a product)
- **Energy bar chart:** E_ch (reactants) is tall; E_ch (products) is shorter; the difference is released as thermal energy (E_th increases).
- **Chemical PE graph:** PE of reactants is **higher** than PE of products. Curve goes up to an activation-energy peak, then drops to a **lower** final PE. ΔPE < 0.

**5. Solid ammonium nitrate dissolves in water — the solution cools.**

Balanced equation (endothermic — solution cools):

$$NH_4NO_3(s) + \text{energy} \rightarrow NH_4^+(aq) + NO_3^-(aq)$$

or equivalently $NH_4NO_3(s) + \text{energy} \rightarrow NH_4NO_3(aq)$

- **Type of energy change: ENDOTHERMIC** (energy is a reactant)
- **Energy bar chart:** E_ch (reactants) is short; E_ch (products) is taller; thermal energy (E_th) of surroundings decreases → beaker feels cold.
- **Chemical PE graph:** PE of reactants is **lower** than PE of products. Curve rises through an activation-energy peak and ends at a **higher** final PE. ΔPE > 0.
